02 / CODEONTO PRIVATE LIMITED

A new operating front for the MHK ecosystem.

Codeonto Private Limited is a unit of MHK Developers, established to bring a focused operating structure to the group's growing portfolio of business and technology-led engagements.

As the MHK platform expanded its work across food & beverage businesses, consulting assignments and technology-led support, Codeonto was established as a dedicated business unit to organize and deliver this growing workstack.

Today, Codeonto operates as a focused technology and intelligence front for modern F&B and FMCG businesses, combining restaurant consulting, marketplace and menu optimization, restaurant technology integration, quick-commerce intelligence and operational analytics.

This structure allows MHK Developers to remain the broader business and strategic platform while Codeonto concentrates on specialized client delivery and technology-enabled execution.
