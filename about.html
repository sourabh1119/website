04 / OUR OPERATING UNIT

Meet Codeonto.

Codeonto Private Limited is a unit of MHK Developers, recently established to manage and deliver the group's growing work across F&B, consulting and technology support services.

It represents a focused client-facing and execution layer within the MHK ecosystem, allowing specialist work to be organized under a dedicated operating identity while remaining connected to the broader MHK Developers platform.

01 / COMPANY PROFILE

Established in 2019.
Built to evolve.

MHK Developers Private Limited is a New Delhi-based private limited company with roots in the construction and renovation sector and an expanding business ecosystem spanning consulting, F&B and technology-led services.

MHK Developers Private Limited was incorporated on 05 February 2019 and is registered with the Registrar of Companies, Delhi under CIN U45202DL2019PTC345466.

The company's original operating focus is connected with alteration, renovation, repair and construction-related services. Over time, the business platform has expanded beyond its construction roots to support a wider range of commercial and entrepreneurial initiatives.

Today, MHK Developers serves as the broader platform through which its business, consulting and development interests are organized. Codeonto Private Limited operates as a dedicated unit within this ecosystem for F&B, consulting and technology-led engagements.
